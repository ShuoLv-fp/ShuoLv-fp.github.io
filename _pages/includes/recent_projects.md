# 🚀 Recent Projects  

**Low-Dimensional Representation-Based Spatiotemporal Dynamics Model of Human Cerebellum**  
  Advisor: Prof. Guoyuan Yang (BIT)  
  - Applied complex principal component analysis for modeling to obtain sparse representations of complex human brain activities.  
  - Verified pattern robustness at the group level and specificity of biological characteristics and behaviors at the individual level.  
  - Accurately described static (zero-lag) and dynamic (time-lag) features of neural activities.  
  - \[Paper\]: 2 submitted papers.  


**Modeling of Social Awareness on Complex Networks**  
  Advisor: Prof. Libo Zhang & Prof. Minyu Feng (SWU)  
  - Designed network dynamics of experts and isolated individuals under disease prevention scenarios based on Prospect Theory and Regret Theory.  
  - Simulated social awareness diffusion on randomly generated networks, demonstrating the promotion effect of human intervention on disease prevention and the containment effect of timely isolation of infected individuals.  
  - \[Paper\]: 2 published papers.  


**Intelligent Security System for Factories**  
  Advisor: Prof. Libo Zhang & Prof. Mingyang Zhong (SWU)  
  - Designed and integrated algorithms for face recognition under abnormal lighting and occlusion, voiceprint recognition in noisy environments, and decision fusion.  
  - Achieved excellent recognition performance in uncontrolled environments.  
  - \[Award\] **🥇 National First Prize** at the 2023 China Robot and Artificial Intelligence Competition.  


**Development of Large Language Model-Based Preschool Education System**  
  Advisor: Prof. Libo Zhang & Rui Chen (SWU)  
  - Designed a generative preschool education system based on vivo's self-developed BlueLM, covering personalized teaching content generation, interactive dialogue, and AI painting teaching.  
  - Provided high-quality and diverse teaching resources.  
  - \[Award\]: **🥇 National First Prize** at the 2024 China Collegiate Computing Contest - AIGC Innovation Track. 


**Development of LLM-Driven Smart Cultural Tourism System** (SWU, 2024)  
  Advisor: Prof. Libo Zhang & Prof. Mingyang Zhong (SWU)  
  - Designed a smart cultural tourism system with wide coverage, optimal planning, and low cost. 
  - Applied big data sentiment analysis for preference mining in information recommendation and VR/AR technology for digital twin explanation.  
  - \[Award\] **🥇 National Special Prize** at the 2024 "Challenge Cup" Competition - "Problem-Solving" Special Track.  


**Chinese Chess Game Model** (SWU, 2021)  
  Advisor: Prof. Shenglin Li (SWU)  
  - Developed a Chinese chess game model using Java, designing search and evaluation functions based on the alpha-beta pruning search tree algorithm.  
  - Implemented personalized features such as human-machine battle mode and chess score saving algorithm.  
  - \[Award\] **🥈 National Second Prize** at the 2022 China Computer Game Championship.  


**Leadership Evaluation Framework Under Polygenic Risk** (BNU, 2024)  
  Advisor: Prof. Ran Li (BNU)
  - Constructed individual leadership portraits covering neural traits, work tendencies, and living habits based on genomic sequencing and questionnaire data of 98 subjects.  
  - Identified several leadership determinant pathways through mediation models.
