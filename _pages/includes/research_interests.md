# 🔬 Research Interests

- <span style="font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.08em;">
  **Spatiotemporal Patterns in Human Brain Dynamics**
  </span>: 
  My research centers on **the neural mechanisms of cognitive function regulation**, integrating AI methods and neuroimaging techniques to analyze human brain dynamics spatially and temporally. I explore these aiming to provide empirical evidence for deciphering brain cognitive modulation mechanisms.  

- <span style="font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.08em;">
  **Complex System**
  </span>: 
  I am also interested in complex systems, focusing on network dynamics and the coupling of multi-agent interactions. I used to employ nonlinear analysis and mathematical modeling to explore evolutionary laws of complex contagion and interactive spreading, striving to offer insights for understanding and regulating complex real-world systems.  

- <span style="font-weight: 600; font-size: 1.08em;">
  **AI Agents for Science (AI4Science)**
  </span>: 
  I am interested in applying LLM-based agents to accelerate scientific discovery and automate research workflows. In the AI4Math direction, I have worked on formal verification and interactive theorem proving (e.g., with Lean), exploring how AI agents can assist in formalizing mathematical statements and automating proof search.
